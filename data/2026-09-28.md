TL;DR: 通过复数化水平速度并以弧长为自变量，得到带恒定升阻力系数旋转球体水平运动的解析解，给出航向角、水平速度和曲率三个精确结果，并将完整飞行问题化为一维标量方程。

Motivation: 旋转球体受马格努斯力侧偏，地面轨迹弯曲；以往结果多依赖数值积分或近似，缺少在升阻力系数恒定时可用于解析分析、并可解释相遇/不相遇等定性问题的闭式框架。

Method: 将水平速度写成复数，以路径长度s替代时间t作自变量；利用两个气动力均为速度二次型，使速度因子在方程中抵消，得到不含重力的常系数线性方程；进一步将完整飞行问题约化为由两个无量纲数控制的一维标量微分方程，并用机器精度数值进行验证。

Result: 航向随单位弧长匀速转动，半圈所需距离与初始条件和阻力无关；水平速度随转向角指数衰减，衰减率由阻力/升力比决定，水平速度端线为对数螺线；地面轨迹曲率半径等于升力长度乘以飞行航迹角余弦；两球对抛对旋在无阻力时可正面相遇，给出单参数闭式族，有阻力时不再相遇。

Conclusion: 在恒定升阻力系数下，旋转球体水平与整体飞行问题具有紧凑的精确解析结构；该结构既解释了几何不变量与相遇条件，也提供了公开代码与数据支持的可复现验证。

Abstract: A ball spinning about a vertical axis is pushed sideways by the Magnus force, so its ground track curves. We show that when the lift and drag coefficients are constant, the horizontal part of this problem is exactly solvable in elementary functions. The step that makes this work is to write the horizontal velocity as a complex number and to use path length rather than time as the independent variable. Both aerodynamic forces are quadratic in speed, so the speed cancels and what remains is a linear equation with constant coefficients in which gravity does not appear. Three exact results follow from it. The heading turns at a fixed rate per unit path length, so a half turn always costs the same distance whatever the launch conditions and whatever the drag. The horizontal speed decays exponentially in the turn angle at a rate fixed by the drag-to-lift ratio, which makes the horizontal hodograph a logarithmic spiral. The radius of curvature of the ground track equals the lift length times the cosine of the flight-path angle. The whole flight problem also collapses to one scalar differential equation controlled by two dimensionless numbers. We use this to settle a concrete question. Two identical balls thrown in opposite horizontal directions with opposite spin can meet head-on when drag is absent, on a one-parameter family we give in closed form, and they fail to meet once drag acts. Every analytic claim is checked against machine-precision numerics, and the code and data are openly available.
