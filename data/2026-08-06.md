Motivation: 实现远距离量子比特之间的纠缠分发是可扩展量子计算的关键要素，但现有方法通常依赖光学腔，复杂度和可扩展性受限，需要开发一种无需腔体且速率可与双量子比特门匹配的量子互连方案。

Method: 利用原子里德伯态间的强偶极-偶极相互作用，在静态量子比特与传播光子之间建立纠缠，避免了对光学腔的依赖；对真实实验参数下的最优纠缠生成协议条件进行了全面分析，并采用镱（Yb）原子的里德伯态进行具体演示。

Result: 在真实实验参数下，该协议可实现纠缠生成速率≥3×10^5 s⁻¹，该速率可与当前中性原子量子处理器的双量子比特门速率相媲美。

Conclusion: 所提出的量子互连具有内在的可扩展性和设计灵活性，为基于中性原子量子架构的分布式量子网络提供了一种有前景的实现途径。

Abstract: Distributing entanglement between distant qubits is a crucial element of scalable quantum computing. Here, we describe a scalable quantum interconnect that generates remote entanglement at rates approaching those compatible with two-qubit gates of current neutral-atom quantum processors. The proposed approach exploits the strong dipole-dipole interactions between atomic Rydberg states to generate entanglement between stationary qubits and propagating photons, without the need for an optical cavity. We provide a thorough description of the optimal conditions for the developed entanglement-generation protocol for realistic experimental parameters and demonstrate that entanglement-generation rates $\gtrsim 3\times 10^5$ s$^{-1}$ can be achieved using Rydberg states of ytterbium atoms. Given the inherent scalability and design flexibility of the proposed interconnect, our results suggest a promising approach towards distributed networks based on neutral-atom quantum architectures.
