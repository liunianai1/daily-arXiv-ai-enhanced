The simulated delay time and group velocity also show good agreement with experiment, with deviations of 2.7% in delay time and 0.9% in group velocity. These results demonstrate the consistency of the time- and frequency-domain analyses and provide a quantitative characterization of the transient propagation behavior of SAW sensors.
